ROOFTOP RUMBLE / SKIPPER

Skipper has been added to the frontend pet catalogue as pet_skipper and to the Achievement Log under Agility > Rooftop Rumble.

Unlock text: Unlock all Graceful items to get Skipper!

The achievement is intentionally locked because Graceful item progression and Rooftop Rumble are not implemented yet. When that system is added, award the pet_skipper ownership key through the same server-side pet ownership data used by the Bank/Pet interface.
