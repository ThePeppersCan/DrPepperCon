REPO COMPANY — QUIDDITCH TCG BINDER BANK FEATURE

Install:
1. Replace web2/script.js with the included version.
2. Copy the included web2/assets/quidditch-tcg-binder folder into your site's matching assets folder.
3. Hard-refresh the website with Ctrl + F5.

Behaviour:
- Adds a QUIDDITCH TCG BINDER button inside the Bank.
- The binder opens on its front cover.
- Clicking the front cover opens the double-page card spread.
- Click the left half of the open binder to return to the front cover.
- Click the right half of the open binder to turn to the back cover.
- Clicking the back cover reopens the binder.
- Previous/Next controls are also included.
- Every page turn plays the supplied sound effect.
- All three images use one fixed responsive display stage, keeping their displayed height consistent.
- The open binder retains its transparent card windows so card artwork can later be layered behind them.
