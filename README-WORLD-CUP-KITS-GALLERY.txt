REPO SPORTS WORLD CUP — 2026 KIT BILLBOARD GALLERY

Install:
1. Drag the contents of this ZIP into the website root.
2. Replace index.html when prompted.
3. Allow the included files to merge into assets/.

What changed:
- The 2026 / 2027 Kits button under each country lore spread now opens a full kit billboard viewer.
- It always starts on the kit belonging to the country lore page you opened it from.
- Left/right controls browse all 16 national kits in World Cup order.
- Arrow keys also browse kits.
- X, Escape, or clicking the dark backdrop returns to the country lore page.
- The existing World Cup page-turn/click sound plays on kit controls.
- Opening uses a restrained centre-out billboard reveal, brightness power-on flicker and one soft sheen.
- Kit switching uses a small directional slide/fade.
- Original kit image files are included without resizing/re-encoding.
