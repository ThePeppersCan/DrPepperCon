GOBLIN BOMB PARTY — MISSED THROW / AI BALANCE FIX

Modified:
- assets/goblin-bomb-party/engine.js

Gameplay changes:
- Missed throws no longer make every goblin run toward the loose bomb.
- Smart goblins actively avoid dangerous grounded bombs.
- Goblins only intentionally collect a loose bomb when it is close, the fuse is safely long, and their personality/AI chooses the risk.
- Reckless/idiot goblins may still make bad decisions occasionally for personality variety.
- A missed throw remains the thrower's responsibility. After a short grace period, the loose bomb homes back toward the player/AI that missed it.
- A red tether warns the player when their missed bomb is returning.
- If a loose bomb explodes far away from everyone, the player/AI responsible for the missed throw is eliminated rather than an unrelated nearest goblin.
- Direct hits still transfer the bomb normally.
- Players can run back over their own loose bomb to recover it before it returns.
- AI now reacts faster, throws more decisively, flanks blocked targets sooner, and Veteran/Insane goblins favour the player more intelligently.
- Normal difficulty received a modest difficulty increase; Veteran and Insane were strengthened further.

No SQL required.
No other minigame files are changed.
